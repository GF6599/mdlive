# Units

## Constants

| Name | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Gravitational constant | $G$ | $6.674 \times 10^{-11}$ m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻² |
| Mass of the sun | $M$ | $1.989 \times 10^{30}$ kg |
| Astronomical unit | AU | $1.496 \times 10^{11}$ m |

## Conversions

- One year is about $3.156 \times 10^7$ seconds.
- One AU is the mean distance from the earth to the sun.
